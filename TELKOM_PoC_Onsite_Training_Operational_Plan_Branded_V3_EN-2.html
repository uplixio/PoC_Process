01PoC Objectives and Training Approach

The training will help TELKOM testers understand the key ixi-O experiences, try the features on their own handsets and provide meaningful feedback. Rather than a presentation-only session, the approach is install → observe a live demonstration → use the service → share feedback.

LG U+ will provide the installation SOP, demonstration devices, feature walkthroughs and technical support. We would welcome the involvement of TELKOM’s key counterparts in facilitating the sessions and assisting participants.

02App Installation and Onboarding

A. Installation using the latest SOP

Participants will use the QR code in the latest SOP shared by LG U+ to download the PoC apps. The QR code may be displayed on a screen or provided in a printed copy of the current SOP. LG U+ will verify the download links before the session.

  • Download the apps: Install the ixi-O main app and the companion call-recording app via the QR code.
  • Grant required permissions: Allow app permissions, adjust unused-app restrictions and set ixi-O as the default phone app.
  • Complete initial onboarding: Review the setup screens, confirm the phone number and open the app.
  • Download on-device AI models: Download the three model files required for functions such as speech-to-text.
  • Configure the companion app: Complete recording permissions and relevant battery optimisation settings.
Important: PoC-only app distribution

For this PoC, the MVP is distributed as an APK through a dedicated testing channel rather than an official app store. As a result, participants must manually grant several permissions and adjust certain settings during installation and first-time setup. These additional steps are specific to the PoC distribution method. For a future commercial release through an official app store, our intention is to provide a substantially simpler setup experience without this level of manual configuration. We will make this distinction clear so that testers do not mistake the PoC installation process for the intended everyday customer experience.

Key message for testers

The current installation process is more involved because this is a test-distributed APK. Please assess the ixi-O service experience separately from these temporary PoC setup requirements. The commercial onboarding experience is intended to be much simpler, subject to final implementation and platform requirements.

B. Network conditions and installation time

During checks at TELKOM’s head office, using both office Wi-Fi and local SIM mobile data, downloads of approximately 160–190 MB took around 10 minutes, and sometimes longer. Participants should therefore allow additional time for app and AI model downloads.

South Africa-specific scam detection optimisation and more open-ended conversational AI Answering should be discussed as future development opportunities, not as confirmed capabilities of this PoC build.

C. Opportunities beyond the PoC

Following the demonstration, we would welcome a discussion about how these capabilities might address TELKOM customer needs. Potential examples include AI-assisted call handling for small businesses, customer enquiries and future B2B service propositions.

The immediate purpose of this PoC is to evaluate the MVP’s usability and customer value. Any broader product scope, commercial proposition or delivery timeline would require separate discussions and agreement.

A. Participant training

We plan to run the training in the sequence hands-on installation → feature demonstration → Q&A, with a target duration of approximately one hour. Timing will remain flexible depending on TELKOM’s network conditions and the time required to install the MVP and its AI models.

Because it is difficult to demonstrate detailed handset interactions remotely, we propose in-person sessions in a TELKOM meeting room, with a handset connected to a display. Additional sessions may be arranged for testers who cannot attend the initial workshop.

B. Demonstration devices and readiness

  • Use the pre-installed handset for a reliable feature demo and a separate unconfigured handset for installation training.
  • Disable Samsung DeX when connecting the handset to the display so that the native mobile app interface is shown.
  • For the scam detection demonstration, check that the calling number is not saved in the receiving handset’s contacts.
  • Retain relevant call history and summaries for AI History, and prepare both everyday conversation and scam-call audio scenarios.

C. Working with TELKOM counterparts

We would appreciate Shadrack and Neo becoming familiar with the installation process, key features and demonstration flow before the workshop. Their participation will help testers follow the session, complete installation and obtain timely assistance.
